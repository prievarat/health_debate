Healthy or Unhealthy?

Classroom debate game based on real-life habits and behaviours.

1. Choose a topic to debate

Click on a card, then decide if you are FOR or AGAINST the habit.

Tip for teachers: assign different topics to pairs or halves of the class.

Game flow

1. Choose topic & side
2. Read the facts
3. Write your own arguments
4. Use Help if needed
5. Speak and debate

Students can switch between Easy and Advanced English at any time.

Are you going to argue that this behaviour is healthy/helpful or unhealthy/harmful?

Tip: you can also ask students to defend the side they personally disagree with.

Facts for your side

Read the facts first. Use them to build your own arguments. If you get stuck, click “Show Help”.

Help: model arguments will use Easy English. You can change the level at the top of the page.

Speaking round

Now debate with your partner or group.

  • Use at least two arguments.
  • Refer to the facts (for example: “One fact says that…”).
  • Try to use linkers: because, so, however, for example.

Reflection

  1. Which argument was the most convincing in your debate?
  2. Did any fact change your opinion about this habit?
  3. Is this habit more healthy or more unhealthy in your view now?
